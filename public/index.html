Quick 3-Minute Installation Guide

Follow these quick visual steps to start auto-replying in minutes.

01

Download & Install APK

Click the download button above. When Chrome prompts "File might be harmful", tap "Download anyway". Open the file and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

02

Grant 2 Android Permissions

Open KYPER:
1. Tap "Grant Accessibility" to enable the floating bubble & typing overlay.
2. Tap "Enable Auto-Reply" to allow Notification Access so KYPER can respond directly to WhatsApp & Insta.

03

Configure & Relax

Set your preferred tone (Savage, Romantic, Respectful) and gender grammar. Tap "Disable Battery Optimizations" so Android doesn't kill the service during deep sleep. You're set!

01

Download & Extract Zip

Download Kyper-extension.zip. Right-click the downloaded zip and choose "Extract All" to a permanent folder on your computer.

02

Load Unpacked Extension

Open Chrome/Brave and navigate to chrome://extensions. Toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner, then click "Load unpacked" and select the extracted folder.

Pin & Enjoy Web AI

Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin KYPER. Open WhatsApp Web or Instagram DM—the floating cyber dock will automatically appear in your chat field!
